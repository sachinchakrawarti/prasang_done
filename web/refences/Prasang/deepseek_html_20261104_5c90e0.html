Viraha — The Pain of Separation

विरह · The soul's longing for the Beloved

From Mirabai's pining for Krishna to Rumi's reed pipe crying for its reed-bed home, viraha is the shared heartbeat of Bhakti and Sufi poetry. It is the ache that opens the heart, the distance that intensifies love. Explore how separation became the most fertile metaphor in South Asian literature.
