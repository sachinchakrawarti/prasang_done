Migration of Ideas

How literary movements traveled across the subcontinent.

The Bhakti Wave

South → North · 7th–17th century

The Bhakti movement began with the Tamil Alvars and Nayanars in the south, then traveled north through Karnataka (Virashaiva), Maharashtra (Varkari), and finally North India (Kabir, Mirabai, Tulsidas).

Tamil Nadu Karnataka Maharashtra North India

The Sufi Trail

Persia → Delhi → Punjab & Bengal · 12th–17th century

Sufi mysticism entered India through the Delhi Sultanate. The Chishti order established khanqahs in Delhi, Ajmer, and later Punjab and Bengal — bringing the ghazal and qawwali to the subcontinent.

Persia Delhi Punjab / Bengal

The Ghazal's Journey

Arabia → Persia → Delhi → Lucknow · 7th–19th century

The ghazal originated in Arabic poetry, matured in Persian, and reached its peak in Urdu under the Mughals. Mir, Sauda, and Ghalib perfected it in Delhi and Lucknow.

Arabia Persia Delhi Lucknow

The Print Revolution

Bengal → North India → Nationwide · 19th–20th century

The printing press and colonial education transformed literature. The Bengal Renaissance sparked a nationwide literary modernization, from Tagore's Bengal to Premchand's Hindi heartland.

Bengal North India Pan-India
