अनहद Anhad

/ən.ˈhəd/ · Noun · Adjective

The Unstruck Sound. The divine sound heard in deep meditation — a sound not produced by any physical instrument, but arising from the silence within. Central to Sufi and Bhakti mysticism, the anhad naad is the primordial vibration of the universe.

Etymology

An-āhata (Sanskrit)

an (not) + āhata (struck) = "unstruck, unbeaten." The sound that is not produced by any impact — the eternal, self-existent vibration.
